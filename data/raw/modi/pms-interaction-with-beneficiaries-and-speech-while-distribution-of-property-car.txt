Namaskar!

Today is a very historic day for the villages of the country, for the rural economy of the country. Honorable Governors of many states are associated with this program. Chief Ministers of Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, UP, Maharashtra and Gujarat have also joined us. Lieutenant Governor of Jammu and Kashmir, Lieutenant Governor of Ladakh are also with us. My colleagues from the Union Cabinet are present in different parts of the country, in different programs. Ministers of state governments are also present, MPs and MLAs are also present, all other public representatives are also present.

All the colleagues associated with thousands of Gram Panchayats, lakhs of beneficiaries of the Swamitva Yojana, this is such a wide and huge program in itself and you all have joined it with great enthusiasm, I congratulate all of you.

Friends,

Swamitva Yojana was started five years ago, so that the people living in the villages could be given legal proof of their house. Somewhere it is called Gharauni, somewhere it is called Adhikar Abhilekh, somewhere it is called Property Card, somewhere it is called Malmatta Patra, somewhere it is called Residential Land Lease. The names are different in different states, but these are certificates of ownership. In the last 5 years, these ownership cards have been given to about 1.5 crore people. Now today, in this program, more than 65 lakh families have received these ownership cards. That is, under the ownership scheme, about 2.25 crore people of the village have received permanent legal documents of their houses. I congratulate all of them, give my best wishes. And because of today’s program, those who have now received their government papers related to the land, how can they take advantage of it, you will definitely get ideas from the conversation I just had.

Friends,

In the 21st century world, there are so many challenges like climate change, water shortage, health crisis, epidemic. But there is another very big challenge in front of the world. This challenge is of property rights, authorized papers of property. Many years ago, the United Nations did a study on land property in many countries of the world. This study revealed that in many countries of the world, people do not have permanent legal documents of property. The United Nations has clearly stated that if poverty is to be reduced, then it is very important for people to have property rights. A leading economist of the world has written an entire book on the challenge of property rights. And in this book, he says that the little property that people have in villages is dead capital. That is, this property is a kind of dead property. Because the villagers, the poor people, cannot do any transaction in exchange for that property. It cannot help in increasing the family’s income.

Friends,

India was also not untouched by this big challenge facing the world. Our situation was also the same. You also know that despite having property worth lakhs and crores of rupees in the villages of India, it was not worth that much. The reason was that people often did not have legal documents of their houses, so there were disputes regarding the ownership of the house. In many places, powerful people would occupy the houses. Without legal documents, even the banks would stay four steps away from such property. This was going on for decades. It would have been good if the previous governments had taken some concrete steps in this direction, but they did not do much in this direction. Therefore, when our government was formed in 2014, we decided to deal with this challenge of property papers, no sensitive government could leave the people of its village in such trouble. And we want everyone’s development, we also want everyone’s trust, our minister Rajiv Ranjan ji explained it very well. Therefore, we started the Swamitva Yojana. We decided that with the help of drones, mapping of the land of houses will be done in every village of the country, villagers will be given papers of their residential property.

Today, when we are seeing the benefits of this scheme, it gives us satisfaction that we were able to work for the poor of the village. I was just talking to the beneficiaries of the Swamitva Yojana. How this scheme has changed their lives, how they are now getting help from banks on their property. You had property, you lived there but there were no papers, the government should have solved that problem and that is why we took up the work and are doing it and I was seeing in their talks the satisfaction on their faces, the happiness, the confidence, the dreams of doing something new, how enjoyable this conversation felt to me, I consider this a great blessing.

Brothers and sisters,

There are more than 6 lakh villages in our country. Nearly half of these villages have been surveyed by drones. After getting legal documents, lakhs of people have taken loans from banks on the basis of their houses and their properties. With this money, they have started their own small business in the village. Many of these are small and medium farmer families. For them, these property cards have become a big guarantee of economic security. Our Dalit, backward and tribal families were the most troubled and affected by illegal encroachments and long disputes in the court. Now, with the availability of legal proof, they are getting relief from this crisis. It is estimated that after the property cards are made in all the villages, the way for economic activity worth more than Rs 100 lakh crore will open. You can imagine how much capital is going to be added to the country’s economy.

Friends,

Today our government is trying to bring Gram Swaraj on the ground with full honesty. With the ownership scheme, the planning and implementation of village development are now improving a lot. Today we will have clear maps, we will know the populated areas, so the planning of development work will also be accurate and we will also get relief from the wastage and obstacles that used to happen due to wrong planning. There are many disputes about which land belongs to the Panchayat and which land is pasture. Now, by getting property rights, the problems of the Gram Panchayats will also be solved and they will also be able to become economically strong. There are incidents of fire in the village, floods, landslides, and many such disasters. By getting property cards, disaster management will be better, it will be easy to get a proper claim in case of disaster.

Friends,

We also know that there are many disputes about the land of farmers. There are difficulties in getting land documents. One has to go to the Patwari again and again, and have to make rounds of the Tehsil. This also opens the way for corruption. To reduce these problems, land records are being digitized. Ownership and Bhu-Aadhaar – these two systems are going to become the basis for the development of villages. Land has also been given a special identity through Bhu-Aadhaar. About 23 crore Bhu-Aadhaar numbers have been issued. With this, it is easy to know which plot belongs to whom. In the last 7-8 years, about 98 percent of land records have been digitized. Maps of most lands are now available digitally.

Friends,

Mahatma Gandhi used to say- India lives in villages, the soul of India is in villages. The realization of this sentiment of Pujya Bapu has been done in the last decade. Most of the more than 2.5 crore families who have received electricity in the last 10 years are from villages. Most of the more than 10 crore families who have received toilets in the last 10 years are also from villages. Most of the 10 crore sisters who have received Ujjwala gas connections live in villages. More than 12 crore families who have received tap water in the last five years are also from villages. Most of the more than 50 crore people who have opened bank accounts are also from villages. More than 1.5 lakh Ayushman Arogya Mandirs have been built in the last decade, and they are mostly in villages, serving the health of the people of the villages. For so many decades after independence, our villages and crores of people in the villages were deprived of such basic facilities. The families of our Dalit, backward and tribal society were the most deprived. Now these families have benefited the most from all these facilities.

Friends,

Unprecedented efforts have been made in the last decade to ensure good roads in villages. In the year 2000, when Atal Bihari Vajpayee was the Prime Minister, the Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana was started. Since then, about 8.25 lakh kilometers of roads have been built in villages. 8.25 lakh kilometers in so many years…. Now you see, in 10 years, we have built almost 4.75 lakh kilometers, that is, almost half of the roads in the last 10 years. Now we are also running the Vibrant Village program to increase connectivity to remote villages located on the border.

And friends,

Not only roads, providing internet to villages has also been our priority. Before 2014, less than 100 panchayats of the country were connected with broadband fiber connection. In the last 10 years, we have connected more than 2 lakh panchayats with broadband fiber connection. Before 2014, there were less than one lakh Common Service Centers in the villages of the country. In the last 10 years, our government has built more than 5 lakh new Common Service Centres. And these are not just figures, with these figures, facilities have reached the villages, modernity has reached. The facilities that people used to see in cities earlier, are now available in the villages. This is not only increasing facilities in the villages, but also economic strength.

Friends,

2025 has also started with big decisions for the villages, for the farmers. The government has decided to continue the Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana. Under this, till now, farmers have received claim amounts of about 1.75 lakh crore rupees, insurance money has been received. Another decision has also been taken regarding DAP fertilizer, whose prices have increased a lot in the world. The government has again made arrangements of thousands of crores of rupees, so that farmers keep getting cheap fertilizers. In the last decade, about 12 lakh crore rupees have been spent to provide cheap fertilizers to the farmers. This is almost double the amount compared to the decade before 2014. Under the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi, about three and a half lakh crore rupees have reached the accounts of farmers so far. This shows the commitment of the Central Government towards farmer welfare.

Friends,

Women power has a very big role in building a developed India. Therefore, in the last decade, we have kept the empowerment of mothers and daughters at the center of every major scheme. Schemes like Bank Sakhi and Bima Sakhi have given new opportunities to women in villages. The Lakhpati Didi Yojana has made more than 1.25 crore women in the country Lakhpati Didis. The Swamitva Yojana has also strengthened the property rights of women. In many states, the names of wives have also been included in the property cards along with the husband. At some places, the first name is the wife’s, while at other places it is the second name, but it has been done with the participation of both. Most of the houses given to the poor under the PM Awas Yojana have also been registered in the name of women. And it is such a pleasant coincidence that the drones of the Swamitva Yojana are also helping in giving property rights to women today. Drones are doing the mapping work in the Swamitva Yojana. With the Namo Drone Didi Yojana, the sisters of the village are becoming drone pilots. They are helping in farming with drones and are earning extra income.

Friends,

With the Swamitva Yojana, our government has given such a capability to the people of the village that can completely transform the rural life of India. If our villages and our poor are empowered, then our journey towards developed India will also be pleasant. Due to the steps taken in the interest of villages and the poor in the last decade, 25 crore people have defeated poverty. I am confident that with schemes like Swamitva, we will be able to make villages strong centers of development. Once again, best wishes to all of you. Thank you!
