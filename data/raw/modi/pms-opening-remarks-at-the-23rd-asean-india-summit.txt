Your Excellency, President Marcos, Your Majesty, Excellencies,

Namaskar!

It is a great pleasure to reconnect once again with our ASEAN family. I warmly congratulate and thank President Marcos for his successful ASEAN Chairmanship and his valuable contribution as India’s Country Coordinator.

This is my thirteenth interaction with ASEAN leaders in this format. Over the past few years, our Act East Policy has given new momentum and greater depth to India-ASEAN relations. Our partnership has now expanded beyond our shared culture and heritage, acquiring new dimensions in areas such as digital technology, defence, and space.

Since 2014, we have undertaken more than 100 initiatives to strengthen people-to-people ties, launched nearly 250 Quick Impact Projects, and provided over 10,000 skill-building scholarships.

Today, direct flights between India and nine ASEAN countries, along with growing tourism, are further evidence of our close ties.

Friends,

Over the past few years, the world has faced one crisis after another. Today, what the world needs most is peace, stability, and predictability. In this context, this year’s theme, “Navigating Our Future, Together,” holds special significance.

Together, India and ASEAN represent nearly one-fourth of the world’s population. That is why it is essential to ensure that our future is shaped not by competition, but by cooperation. We have both the capabilities and the shared resolve to achieve this. Together, we can make this region a symbol of peace and stability.

Friends,

This is my third visit to an ASEAN country this year, reflecting the special importance ASEAN holds for India. We firmly believe that India’s growth and ASEAN’s prosperity are deeply interconnected.

In the coming years, both the ASEAN Community Vision 2045 and Viksit Bharat 2047 can help us turn our shared aspirations into reality.

Over the past twelve years, we have given fresh momentum to our partnership. Now, together, we must give a new direction to the future of our region. India’s commitment to ASEAN unity and centrality in this partnership has been, and will remain, unwavering.

I am confident that today’s meeting will take our Comprehensive Strategic Partnership to new heights and contribute to building a secure, inclusive, and prosperous Indo-Pacific.

I look forward to hearing your views and suggestions.

Thank you very much.
